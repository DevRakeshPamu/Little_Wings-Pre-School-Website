Preschools run on scattered tools

Owners

Little daily visibility. Fees and attendance tracked by hand. Hard to manage branches.

Teachers

Repeated paperwork. Photos shared by hand. The same update sent to many parents.

Parents

Few clues about the child’s day. Updates arrive in different places at different times.

Paper registers, WhatsApp groups and Excel sheets, none of them connected.

Today most Indian preschools depend on paper attendance, WhatsApp photo groups and spreadsheets for fees and records.
