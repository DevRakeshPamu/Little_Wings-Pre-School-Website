A 45-day paid pilot to prove it

Weeks 1–2

Onboard the centre, import children, train teachers, start attendance and journeys.

Week 3

Invite parents by web link, WhatsApp summary or app. Track weekly engagement.

Week 4+

Review adoption, interview owners, and decide: pay and continue, or stop.

Pass bar: 3 centres, 80% weekly active teachers, 60% parent engagement, 2 centres paying.

Targets are hypotheses. Do not scale until recurring use and willingness to pay are shown.
