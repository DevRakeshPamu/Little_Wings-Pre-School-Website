About us

Small groups, warm teachers, clear updates

Sunshine Kids teaches through play, stories, music and hands-on activities. Teachers note each child’s progress in everyday words, and parents get a daily summary with photos you approve.

Learning through play

Colours, letters, numbers and shapes taught in short, joyful activities.

Safe pickup

Children go home only with people on your authorized list, and you get a notification each time.

Healthy meals

A weekly menu shared in advance, with allergies and diets noted for each child.

Parents in the loop

Events, holidays and reminders arrive in one place instead of scattered chat groups.

Programs

Find the right group for your child

Timings are 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Daycare hours can be extended on request.

1½ to 2½ years

Playgroup

Gentle first steps: songs, sensory play and settling in with a caring adult.

2½ to 3½ years

Nursery

Colours, early words, sharing and self-care in a play-led day.

3½ to 4½ years

LKG

Phonics, numbers and storytelling, with plenty of movement.

4½ to 5½ years

UKG

Reading and counting readiness, projects and school preparation.

1½ to 5½ years

Daycare

Safe, caring extended hours with meals, rest and play.
